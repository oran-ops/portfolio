The hand for the sticky notes

Five candidates, all free for commercial use and all licensed to be embedded — which matters, because the document makes no external request and the face has to be inlined like every other asset. Each is subset to the letters the notes can ever need, which is the difference between a 51 KB font and a 6 KB one. Total for all five here: 70 KB; only one ships.

The notes are at the size they will really be on a disk. A hand that reads beautifully large can be mud at 15 px, and small is the only size that matters.

My pick is Permanent Marker: a note stuck to a floppy disk was written with whatever was on the desk, and that was a marker. It also holds up smallest — the strokes stay open at 15 px where the pen hands start to fill in.

If you want the pen rather than the marker, Caveat is the one: it is the most natural of them and the least like a typeface. Say which and I will inline it and build the note around it.
