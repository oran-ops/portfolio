HOW I LEAD · PRINCIPLES & TOOLKIT
Leadership▸ DOCTRINE
Great leaders build people who no longer depend on them.
ALEADERSHIP PRINCIPLES
01
Build Trust Before Performance
People perform better when expectations are clear and trust is earned.
02
Coach Before You Judge
Every performance issue deserves investigation first — understand the process, review the data, listen first, coach second, decide last.
03
Create Ownership
People shouldn't execute tasks. They should own outcomes.
04
Decisions Based on Facts
KPIs don't replace leadership — they improve it. Every coaching session begins with evidence, not assumptions.
05
Great Managers Solve Today's Problems. Great Leaders Build Tomorrow's System.
My objective was never to close one more deal — it was to build an organization that produces consistent results without depending on one individual.
